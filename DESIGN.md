# DESIGN — The Nursing Journey – Siva Naik

> Context file for the Impeccable design skill. Describes the intended visual
> identity so design/critique commands judge against the brand, not generic web
> averages.

## Concept

A **space-themed nursing journey**: a deep-navy "space" canvas with a glowing
neon-blue ring and a **gold-orange gradient** used for headings and CTAs — an
echo of the circular logo badge (navy starfield, neon ring, Earth + nurse cap +
red cross, orbital gold rocket). The site must feel **premium, confident,
trustworthy** — a polished product landing page, not a generic template.

## Colour tokens (extracted from the logo)

- **Deep Space Navy** (dark sections): `#050B1F → #0A1330` gradient
- **Neon Ring Blue** (glow, borders, hover): `#3FA9F5` / `#63C7FF`
- **Gold-Orange Gradient** (headings, CTA, "Journey" accents): `#F5C542 → #E8622C`
- **White**: primary text on dark backgrounds
- **Red Cross accent** (sparingly, medical/urgent only): `#E63946`
- **Light sections**: `#F7F9FC` bg, `#0B1F4D` text
- **Service cards** (established identifiers): Studies `#2E9E4F`, Council `#1E63C4`, Career `#6B3FA0`

## Typography

- Headings: **Poppins 600–800** (`font-display`)
- Body: **Inter 400–700**
- Section headlines echo the logo's split-colour treatment (white/navy + a
  gold-orange gradient on the accent word).

## Layout & components

- One page, anchor-linked: Home, About, Services, Resources, Why Us, Contact.
- Hero: bold headline, trust stats, primary CTA, nurse portrait on the right.
- Service cards in the 3 brand colours.
- Subtle starfield / glow-ring motif on hero + footer (echoes the logo) — used
  sparingly, never noise.
- Fully responsive; mobile-first.

## Rules / anti-patterns to avoid

- Keep the navy/gold/neon palette dominant; green/blue/purple only on service cards.
- Motion: subtle and purposeful (Reveal on scroll), never distracting or bouncy.
- Avoid nested-card slop, generic purple gradients, and flat "Inter for
  everything" boilerplate.
- Keep text readable on dark surfaces (no grey-on-colour traps).
