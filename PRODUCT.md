## What this is

A **one-page marketing website** for **The Nursing Journey – Siva Naik**, a
nursing consultancy and education brand based in Andhra Pradesh & Telangana,
India, founded by **Siva Naik** — an ICU Staff Nurse, BSc Nursing graduate, and
AIIMS NORCET exam-prep content creator.

- Tagline: _"Your Trusted Partner in Every Step of Your Nursing Journey"_
- Process line: **STUDY • REGISTER • GROW • SERVE**
- Hero quote: _"From Nursing Studies to Professional Success — We Support Your
  Journey."_

## Audience

- Nursing students (BSc / GNM / Post Basic) in India, especially AP & Telangana.
- Students preparing for **AIIMS NORCET** and other nursing entrance/recruitment
  exams.
- Practising nurses and graduates needing council registration, renewals,
  documentation and career guidance — including overseas opportunities.

## Core service pillars

1. **Studies & Education** — notes, MCQs, exam prep, study materials.
2. **Council Services** — nursing council registration & renewals, documentation.
3. **Career Support** — job / opportunity guidance and next steps.

## Voice & tone

Warm, credible, practical. A real nurse and educator talking to students — not a
faceless agency. Emphasise reliability, confidentiality and step-by-step
support. Avoid salesy pressure and hype.
